## Objective
Create a web app to listen to audio recordings of a narrated book. An image view will be at center focus of the app, and below will be a text commentary box and scrubbable timeline. The images and commentary will be timestamped and will be shown at the relevant timestamp of the recording.

## Required features
- Separate app logic from content, include ways to write content in MD or some other plain text format
- pause/play button
- different types of commentary, clarifications, asides, and additions
- scrubbable timeline with color coded indicators for the commentary. Hovering commentary should provide a preview
